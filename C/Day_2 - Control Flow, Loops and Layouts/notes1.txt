B. Type Modifiers
   -- Sign Modifiers (Changes alignment behavior around zero)
      - signed   : Splits range evenly between positive and negative realms.
                   Example: -128 to +127.
      - unsigned : Eliminates negative space entirely. Capacity begins at 0.
                   Example: 0 to 255.
   -- Size Modifiers (Changes raw memory allocation)
      - short    : Restricts capacity allocation (Typical: 2 Bytes / 16 bits).
      - long     : Expands capacity allocation (Typical: 4 or 8 Bytes).
